About Okai Paul Elisha

Okai Paul Elisha is a business owner, software engineer, computer engineer and hardware specialist. Okai is the founder of Ulisha Limited, the company behind the online store Ulisha Store and the farming product U-Farm, and the founder of X-Station, a modern solar charging solution. Okai also owns Lisa Premium Export, an export company that ships products such as coal and charcoal.

Okai's work covers the full stack of a technology business: designing and repairing hardware, building solar and battery power systems, writing software for web and mobile, and running the operations, sales and customer service that keep a company growing.

How I work

The same five steps whether the job is a storefront, a firmware fix or a dead motherboard.

  1. Diagnose

    Find the real problem before touching anything.

  2. Design

    Choose the simplest fix in hardware, software or process.

  3. Build

    Write the code, wire the board, set up the system.

  4. Test

    Check it under real load before it reaches you.

  5. Support

    Stay on call after handover. The business depends on it.
